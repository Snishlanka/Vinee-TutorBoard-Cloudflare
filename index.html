What's new in your teaching studio

A refreshed teaching workspace

More room for the board, responsive tools, refined light and dark themes, and floating text and chart formatting.

Editable tables and statistics

Turn class boundaries and frequencies into histograms, frequency polygons and smooth cumulative curves. Add editable tables with flexible backgrounds.

Teach quartiles step by step

Explain Q1, Median and Q3 by revealing cumulative frequency levels, reading guides and coordinates one step at a time.

Read any value from a graph

Enter X to find Y, or Y to find X, on function graphs and cumulative curves. Show guides and coordinates to make each reading clear.

BUILT FOR TEACHING

A practical workspace for tutors and teachers

Use it while screen sharing in an online class, or prepare a visual lesson in advance. It works well for mathematics, accounting explanations and step-by-step exam question discussions.

Natural board tools

Mathematics tools
